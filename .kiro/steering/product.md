# Product: Lazarus Reader

- Kiroween 2026 entry, category: **Resurrection** (revive dead tech with modern innovation).
- Resurrects the classic RSS reader (Google Reader, shut down 2013-07-01).
- Audience: people who want a calm, ad-free, algorithm-free news feed they own.
- Principles: self-hosted, private, fast, keyboard-first, zero lock-in (OPML in/out).
- Tone: spooky Halloween styling, but reading comfort always wins.
